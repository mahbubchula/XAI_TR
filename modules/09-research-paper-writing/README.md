- Modules 01-08: All previous modules
- Completed ML project with results

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Structure a research paper featuring ML/AI methods
- Write clear methodology sections for ML research
- Present results with appropriate visualizations and statistics
- Discuss model interpretability and limitations
- Follow publication standards for your field
- Address common reviewer concerns about AI methods
